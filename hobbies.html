Personal appendix

What keeps me curious.

Engineering is part of my life, not the whole thing. These are the interests that reset my attention and often send me back to work with a better question.

01

Astrophotography

Long exposures reward patient setup and repeatable process. I built AstroPipeline in the aporn-tool repository because restarting multi-stage media work by hand was a problem worth removing.

02

Music and singing

A different kind of iteration: listen closely, adjust, and learn when not to overwork the result.

03

Gaming

I enjoy systems that teach through feedback, especially when a difficult mechanic becomes readable through practice.

04

Coffee sessions

Good coffee and an unhurried conversation remain one of the best ways to untangle a fuzzy problem.

05

Hiking and the outdoors

Time outdoors creates distance from the screen and restores the kind of attention that debugging tends to consume.
